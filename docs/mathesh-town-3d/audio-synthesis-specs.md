# TownAudio Procedural Web Audio Synthesis Specifications

- **Car Engine**: Triangle wave oscillator modulated from 48Hz to 158Hz based on speed.
- **Dual-Tone Horn**: Simultaneous 440Hz & 554.37Hz sawtooth oscillators with low-pass filter.
- **Speed Camera Shutter**: Decaying white noise burst + 1400Hz to 2600Hz frequency sweep.
- **Drift Screech**: Bandpass filtered modulated sawtooth noise.
